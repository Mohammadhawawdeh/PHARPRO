Company profile

A specialist partner for complex compliance work

PHARPRO Consultation Company L.L.C FZ was established in the UAE in 2023. Its consulting delivery is led from Amman, Jordan, supporting pharmaceutical manufacturers, distributors and regulated technology teams across Jordan, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt and the wider MENA region. The company focuses on assignments where quality, engineering and digital systems must produce one defensible evidence package—not separate documents that fail to connect during an inspection.

Computerized System Validation

Risk-based CSV strategy, URS, validation planning, IQ/OQ/PQ, requirements traceability, audit-trail review and validation closure aligned with GAMP 5, EU GMP Annex 11 and FDA 21 CFR Part 11.

Quality Assurance and Inspection Readiness

GMP gap assessments, data-integrity reviews, CAPA, SOP systems, mock audits and remediation plans designed around the applicable authority, product and operating model.

CQV and Thermal Mapping

Qualification of equipment, HVAC, utilities, warehouses and cold rooms, including DQ/IQ/OQ/PQ, risk-based protocols and clear acceptance evidence.

Training and Digital Validation

Practical GMP and CSV training in English or Arabic, plus PHARPRO DVS for controlled, AI-assisted validation document workflows with human review and approval.

How we work

Evidence first. Clear scope. No compliance theatre.

PHARPRO’s role is to identify risk, build usable controls and make the resulting evidence easy to defend. Regulatory claims are always evaluated against the client’s actual product, process, system and target market.

1. Define the decision

Agree the regulatory scope, intended use, responsibilities, acceptance criteria and final decision owner before creating documents.

2. Follow the risk

Prioritise patient safety, product quality and data integrity so effort is concentrated where failure has the greatest impact.

3. Leave a usable system

Deliver controlled documents, traceable evidence and knowledge transfer that the client’s own quality team can maintain after project closure.
