The calculations

Transparent formulas, no black box

Every formula is visible in the workbook and referenced to current guidance, so your reviewers and inspectors can follow each number.

MACO (HBEL) = PDEA × MBSB ÷ MDDB
MACO (dose) = TDDA × MBSB ÷ (SF × MDDB) SF = 1000 by default
MACO (10 ppm) = 10 mg/kg × MBSB
Surface limit = MACO ÷ shared surface area → swab limit = surface limit × swab area

A = previous product, B = next product, PDE = permitted daily exposure, TDD = minimum therapeutic daily dose, MDD = maximum daily dose, MBS = minimum batch size. Read the background in our MACO and acceptance criteria guide.

Frequently asked questions

MACO calculator FAQs

Which MACO methods does the calculator use?

Health-based exposure limits (HBEL/PDE), the 1/1000 minimum therapeutic dose criterion and the 10 ppm criterion. Each method can be switched on or off, and the final MACO is the lowest of the methods you enable.

Is the spreadsheet validated?

It is built to be validated. A Verification sheet re-derives the example calculation by hand and shows PASS or FAIL for each step, giving you objective evidence for your own spreadsheet validation under EU GMP Annex 11.

Does it include PDE values?

No. PDEs must come from a toxicological assessment for your actives. The example rows use illustrative values only. PHARPRO can help you source or commission PDE monographs.
