What's inside

Seven SOPs, nineteen forms, one consistent system

Every SOP has purpose, scope, responsibilities, definitions, references, step-by-step procedure, flowchart and change record, and every form uses the same numbering and CSRA reference, so the documents work together from day one.

SOP-VA-005 · User Requirements (CSURS)

Requirement categories from operational to 21 CFR Part 11, with a detailed form that already lists about 140 typical requirements, including Part 11 and Annex 11 controls for audit trails, access, e-signatures, cybersecurity and cloud services, a simplified form and a tracking logbook.

SOP-VA-006 · Traceability Matrix (CSTM)

Links every approved requirement to its test, so gaps between requirements and testing are visible before the inspector finds them. Form and logbook included.

SOP-VA-008 · GxP Determination

Decides whether a system is GxP critical, records hardware and software details and assigns the CSRA reference number used through the whole life cycle.

SOP-VA-009 · Impact Assessment

Structured impact questions on product quality, patient safety and data integrity, with risk assessment and residual-risk forms.

SOP-VA-010 · GAMP Categorization

Breaks the system into components, assigns GAMP 5 Category 1, 3, 4 or 5 and defines the supplier documents and validation effort required.

SOP-VA-011 · Functional Risk Assessment

Severity, probability and detectability scoring of each required function of Category 4 and 5 systems, with controls, residual-risk report and register.

SOP-VA-012 · Supplier Risk Assessment

Risk-based choice between a basic assessment, a postal or email questionnaire and an on-site or virtual audit, with a ready supplier questionnaire, audit form and tracking list.

How it fits together

Follows the GAMP 5 risk-based life cycle

Use the SOPs in sequence for a new system, or start at any step to bring legacy systems into compliance. Read the background in our CSV and data integrity insights.

1. Define · Write the CSURS before you buy or build (SOP-VA-005).
2. Determine · Decide if the system is GxP and give it a CSRA number (SOP-VA-008).
3. Assess impact · Assess impact and initial risks for GxP-critical systems (SOP-VA-009).
4. Categorize · Assign the GAMP 5 category to scale the validation effort (SOP-VA-010).
5. Assess functions · Run a functional risk assessment for Category 4 and 5 systems (SOP-VA-011).
6. Qualify the supplier · Assess or audit the supplier according to system category and impact (SOP-VA-012).
7. Trace · Prove every requirement is tested with the traceability matrix (SOP-VA-006).
