Announcement: PHARPRO Completes the First Half of a GSDP Project for a Nuclear Pharmaceutical Warehouse in Amman, Jordan

PHARPRO has completed the first half of a Good Storage and Distribution Practices (GSDP) project for a nuclear pharmaceutical warehouse in Amman, Jordan. The documentation, mapping and qualification work is finished, and the project has now moved into the training phase.

By Mohammad Awawdeh · Published 24 November 2026

What has been completed

The first half of the project built the GSDP foundation the warehouse needs before routine operation:

  • GSDP SOPs: a set of standard operating procedures covering receipt, storage, temperature control, dispatch, deviations and the other GSDP activities of the warehouse.
  • Layouts: warehouse layouts that define storage zones and the flow of goods and people through the site.
  • Temperature mapping: mapping of the storage areas to show where temperatures can vary and where monitoring probes belong.
  • Refrigerator and freezer qualification: qualification of the cold-storage equipment used for temperature-sensitive products.
  • Transport qualification: qualification of the transport arrangements used to move products from the warehouse while keeping them within their required conditions.

Each deliverable is documented so the client's quality team can show how storage and distribution conditions are defined, verified and controlled.

Now in progress: training

The project has now moved into the training phase. Procedures and qualified equipment only protect product quality when the people using them understand what is required, so PHARPRO is training the warehouse team on the new GSDP SOPs and the practical controls behind them.

Why GSDP matters for nuclear pharmaceuticals

Nuclear pharmaceuticals combine time- and temperature-sensitive handling with radiation-protection controls. That makes storage layout, cold-chain equipment, transport arrangements and staff training more demanding than in a conventional pharmaceutical warehouse, and it is why each element has to be designed and qualified rather than assumed.

Frequently asked questions

What is GSDP?

Good Storage and Distribution Practices (GSDP) are the requirements that keep medicines within their approved conditions from receipt in a warehouse to delivery. They cover the quality system, premises, temperature control, equipment qualification, transport and personnel training.

Which GSDP deliverables are already complete?

The GSDP SOPs, warehouse layouts, temperature mapping, refrigerator and freezer qualification, and transport qualification are complete. The project is now in the training phase.

Why does a nuclear pharmaceutical warehouse need its own GSDP approach?

Nuclear pharmaceuticals combine time- and temperature-sensitive handling with radiation-protection controls, so storage areas, cold-chain equipment, transport routes and staff training have to be designed and qualified with both sets of requirements in mind.
