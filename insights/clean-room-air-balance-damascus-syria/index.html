PHARPRO Completes Clean Room Air Balance Execution, Testing and Documentation in Damascus, Syria

PHARPRO has completed the air balance workstream - execution, testing and documentation - for a clean room suite at a pharmaceutical client site in Damascus, Syria, as part of an active regional turnkey validation engagement.

By Mohammad Awawdeh · Published 22 September 2026

Scope of the air balance workstream

Air balance is the foundation that every later cleanroom qualification step depends on. Before HEPA integrity, recovery time or particle classification can mean anything, the supply and return airflow volumes have to be measured, adjusted and confirmed against the approved design - and the pressure cascade between adjacent rooms has to hold under real operating conditions, not just on paper.

PHARPRO's team executed the full air balance scope at the client's Damascus site: measuring and adjusting supply and return air volumes at each diffuser and grille, verifying differential pressure across the room cascade, confirming air change rates against design intent, and compiling the execution into a documented, traceable package ready to support the site's broader qualification file.

Why this matters before the next step

An unbalanced system produces qualification failures that look like equipment problems but are actually airflow problems - inconsistent particle counts, pressure excursions during door openings, or recovery times that vary by test day. Getting air balance right first, with clear documentation of what was measured and what was adjusted, is what makes every subsequent test in the qualification sequence defensible.

The completed package gives the client's quality team traceable evidence connecting design intent to as-built performance - exactly what an inspector will expect to see referenced when reviewing the room's qualification history.
