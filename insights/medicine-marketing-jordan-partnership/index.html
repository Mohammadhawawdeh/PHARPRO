Marketing That Stays Within Regulatory Bounds

Pharmaceutical promotion in Jordan is regulated by JFDA and must comply with IFPMA/EFPIA codes of practice for interactions with healthcare professionals. PHARPRO's involvement ensures that every marketing activity is reviewed for compliance with applicable codes — protecting your product licence and your organisation's reputation.

  • Promotional materials reviewed for JFDA compliance before use
  • HCP engagements structured to meet transparency and documentation requirements
  • Medical information function support available alongside promotional activities
  • Clear separation maintained between promotional and scientific/educational activities
