Privacy Notice

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Who this notice covers

This notice applies to information submitted through pharpro.co and its contact, assessment, registration and resource-download forms. The website is operated by PHARPRO Consultation Company L.L.C FZ.

Information we collect

Depending on the form you use, PHARPRO may collect your name, work email, telephone or WhatsApp number, company, country, service interest and the project information you choose to provide. We also use essential technical information needed to deliver and secure the website.

Why we use it

  • To respond to enquiries, arrange calls and prepare relevant proposals.
  • To deliver a requested checklist, training confirmation or other resource.
  • To maintain business records and protect the website from spam or misuse.
  • To understand website performance when you consent to analytics cookies.

Service providers

Form submissions may be processed through PHARPRO's configured form-delivery provider. If you choose WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Calendly or another external service, that provider processes information under its own privacy terms. Google Analytics is loaded only after analytics consent is accepted on this website.

How long information is kept

Enquiry information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage the potential or active business relationship, meet legal or accounting requirements and resolve disputes. PHARPRO does not sell website enquiry information.

Your choices

You may decline analytics cookies and continue using the website. You may also ask PHARPRO to correct or delete enquiry information, subject to legal or contractual record-keeping obligations.

Contact

For a privacy question or request, email info@pharpro.co. Please do not include confidential product, patient or system credentials in a website form.
